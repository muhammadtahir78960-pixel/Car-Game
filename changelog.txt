🚗 What's New in Car Game v2.5! 🚀
​📅 Last Update Date: Sept 03, 2026
​We are thrilled to bring you a massive update packed with exciting new features, gameplay enhancements, and crucial bug fixes!
​🔥 Engine Temperature & Cooling System (Racing Mode)
​Overheating Challenge: Car engine temperature mechanics added to Racing Mode for a more realistic and challenging drive!
​New Cooling Items: Cool down your engine using new inventory items—now available for purchase from Mechanic Leo at the Garage.
​🏎️ Offline Racing Lobby & Screen Reader Mode
​Enhanced Lobby: Improved the Offline Racing Lobby interface for a smoother experience.
​Screen Reader Mode: Introduced a dedicated Screen Reader Mode (Note: Winning rewards for this mode are slightly reduced).
​🕹️ Gameplay Improvements
​Idle Safety: Crashes and Zombie Kills will no longer trigger while in idle condition.
​Honking Traffic: Selected obstacle cars will now blow their horn in traffic!
​🏆 Tournament Mode Enhancements
​Player Status: Real-time Online/Offline status indicators added to the Tournament Lobby.
​Vice Captain Role: Highly requested Vice Captain feature is now officially implemented!
​Creator Auto-Join: Tournament creators can now use the new Auto-Join checkbox when hosting.
​🎁 New Gifting System
​Send and receive online gifts directly with your in-game friends!
​💬 Global Chat Upgrades
​Better Stability: Improved screen stability for a lag-free chatting experience.
​One-Click Clear: Instantly clear all kept messages with a single tap.
​Higher Character Limit: Express yourself better with increased message character limits.
​🔐 Login, Registration & Support
​Enhanced Stability: Login and Registration screens are now much more reliable.
​Direct Support: Added Developer WhatsApp Contact, Official WhatsApp Gaming Group, and Developer Email in the About section.
​🏪 UI, Accessibility & Bug Fixes
​Shop Layout: Redesigned Shop UI for a sleek and smooth browsing experience.
​Audio & Accessibility: Better overall accessibility support paired with brand-new SFX.
​Bug Fixes: Resolved issues across Online Racing, Reaction Mode, and Player Stats.
​Update your game now and enjoy the ultimate driving experience! 🚘💥
